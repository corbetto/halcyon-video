# Halcyon Video 0.23.0

You can now record a store walkthrough from your controller, including browsing, case inspection and checkout. The video saves locally, and you can start another take without leaving the store. Recordings are silent.

## In this release

- **Store recording:** opt-in Reel Recording mode, smooth flight controls and continuous recording through checkout and the store terminal. Navigation stays usable while recording, and typing in search no longer triggers recording shortcuts.
- **Rental details:** recorded VHS clamshell and DVD keepcase sounds for handling and case turns, VHS-style store-copy artwork for standard DVD wraps, and fuller game shelves using the same modeled shelf parts as the rest of the store. Display copies do not add fake catalog titles.
- **More store detail:** modeled fluorescent housings, ceiling ventilation diffusers and candy cartons, plus hanging snack pouches on the public rack and gondola. Entrance, returns-window and cooler details have also been corrected.
- **Reliability:** background graphics preparation pauses for movement, streaming-only desktop stores survive reload, controller navigation works in the flat catalog, rented games use their configured launchers, returning from rentals preserves home navigation, and the power menu no longer overlaps playback during checkout handoff. Several rendering and texture-upload costs have been reduced.

## Still being worked on

This release does not establish the five-second phone-entry target or hitch-free performance on physical phones. Large libraries and high-quality moving-camera recordings can still be slow. The rendering follow-up remains open in issue 373, alongside the mobile performance work.

The separate mobile catalog has fixture-tested publishing, browsing, deployment and linking foundations on dev. It is not a launched live catalog: source permissions, live data, hosting and device acceptance remain gated.

## Try it

[Open the browser store](https://halcyon-video.github.io/halcyon-video/) without a Halcyon account or media server. Streaming providers may require their own subscriptions and sign-in; watch-options links are not a promise of playback.

For your own Jellyfin or Plex library, use the self-hosted app. The Docker release is `ghcr.io/halcyon-video/halcyon-video:0.23.0`, also available as `:latest`.
